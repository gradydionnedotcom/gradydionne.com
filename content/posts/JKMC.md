As an audio engineer for Jordan Kahn Music Company, I am the sole audio providor through setup, soundcheck, front of house mixing, monitor mixing, and tear down. Integrating the company system in any room standalone or with existing audio equipment, I provide music to keep clients dancing all night, from wedding receptions to corporate mixers. 

I work with a Midas M32 digital mixer controlled from an iPad, Sennheiser and Shure wireless microphones and wireless IEM transmitters, and a fully miked band, typically consisting of 11 members.
