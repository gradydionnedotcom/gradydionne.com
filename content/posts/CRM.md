If you're interested in booking guitar lessons, you can fill out the contact formin the top right corner. You can also find me at Castle Rock Music [here](https://castlerockmusic.com/lessons/guitar/), and email me at grady@castlerockmusic.com

As an educator, I strive to meet students where they're at, whether it's their first time picking up the instrument or they're coming to me to get past a progress plateau later in their development. I love and appreciate all genres of music, but most importantly I want music lessons to be the part of your day that you look forward to. There's no set path, each lesson is tailored to the student's interests and curiosities
