If you're interested in learning more about Macedonia Baptist Church Denver, you can find their website [here](https://www.macedoniadenver.org/)

As the in house engineer at Macedonia Baptist Church, I work directly with church staff, speakers, band, and choir members to create a seamless interaction with the audio system . I strive for clear speech and engaging music from FOH,  an accurate transmission to the weekly livestream, and clean in-ear mixes for the band.

I work with an Allen and Heath QU-5 mixer, Shure 58 wireless mics, and a fully miked band
